Our process

How do we build your ideal website?

01

Discovery and agreement

We learn about your business and audience, choose a suitable package and collect your images and copy. Together we define the website's goal, key pages and the action you want visitors to take. We agree on the project scope before development begins.

02

Design and architecture

We create the visual identity and page structure around a smooth user experience. Colours, typography and layouts align with your brand. We start with what matters most to your future clients.

03

Development

We write clean code, build the site's features and optimise it for mobile devices and loading speed. We connect contact forms and agreed functionality. Animation helps visitors find their way and gives your brand character.

04

Testing and launch

After your approval, we connect your domain and launch your website. We check links, the contact form and layouts on different screens. After launch, we explain the basics of managing your website.

Questions, answered

Good collaboration starts with clear answers.

You do not need to know web technology. We make the process easy to understand, from the first idea to your published website.

How long does a website take to build?

The timeline depends on the package, features and the availability of your copy and photographs. After our first conversation, we define the scope and agree on a realistic deadline before work begins.

What should I prepare?

Start with a description of your business and what you want to achieve. Your logo, copy, photographs and examples of websites you like help us establish the right direction sooner.

Are hosting and a domain included?

Hosting and domains are not included in package prices. We help you choose a suitable solution and connect your domain, with costs agreed in advance.

Can I expand my website later?

Yes. We can add pages, languages or features. We first agree together on the scope and price of each addition.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We review your existing content, structure and device layouts, then suggest what to keep and what to improve in line with your goals.

Is the consultation free?

The initial consultation is free and comes with no obligation. We discuss your needs and recommend a package that makes sense for your business.

Are the packages fixed or can they be customised?

All packages are flexible and can be tailored to your needs. We agree on the number of pages, features and content together, and set the final price based on the agreed scope before work begins.
